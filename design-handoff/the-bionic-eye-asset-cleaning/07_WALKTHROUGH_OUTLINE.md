# 07 · Walkthrough speaking outline (target 2 min 30 s)

**Status: for the human entrant.** The contest requires a 2–3 minute screen recording **in your own voice**, navigating the **actual Figma file**. This outline is a prompt sheet, not a script to be read by a synthetic voice — an AI-generated narration would not meet the requirement.

Record at 1080p or higher, Figma at 100 % zoom on each frame, cursor visible. Rehearse once; aim for 2:20–2:40.

| Time | Show in Figma | Say (in your own words) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Cover page, then the page list | Who you are; the brief: an Asset Cleaning page for The Bionic Eye, dealer for ABZ Innovation, featuring the C10. How the file is organised. |
| 0:15–0:40 | 01 Foundations: logo, colour styles, type styles | Colour comes from the client's own material — the logo ink and the blue of their video title card — with neutrals so the footage carries the colour. Typeface is Archivo, the face on their C10 datasheet, using its width axis instead of a second family. Contrast checked to WCAG AA. |
| 0:40–1:05 | Desktop frame → Header and 01 Hero | Indexed sections like a technical manual. Headline and introduction are verbatim from the brochure. The named film is the hero; its title cards become four chapters, and the controls sit below the frame so titles and watermarks are never covered. "Book a demonstration" appears in the header, hero and form. |
| 1:05–1:25 | 02 Applications | The four applications come straight from the film; each tile jumps the hero film to that moment. Real footage, including UK phone footage shot vertically to show reach. |
| 1:25–1:50 | 03 The system → 03.1 Specifications | Ink band for the technology story, with the introduction film. The spec panel reproduces the datasheet exactly, in tabular figures, with the PDF one click away. Headline figures are only values no other source contradicts — mention the conflicts you flagged. |
| 1:50–2:10 | 04 Support and 05 Operating legally | The five services exactly as named; no descriptions invented. Training, ground-unit and van films sit with the services they evidence. The regulatory note cites its source and is marked pending for UK wording rather than implying approval. |
| 2:10–2:30 | 06 Enquiry, Footer, then Mobile 390 frames | A short form with fields marked as proposals pending approval. Show the mobile hero, edge-to-edge film and collapsible specification. Close on WordPress: tokens map to theme.json, sections to block patterns, video to the core Video block. |

Before submitting: check the Figma link opens in a private window with "can view" access, and that the recording is between 2:00 and 3:00.
