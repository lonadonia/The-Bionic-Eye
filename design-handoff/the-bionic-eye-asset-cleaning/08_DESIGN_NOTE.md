# 08 · Design note (typeface and colour)

<!-- note:start -->
Typeface — Manrope for headlines, figures and labels; DM Sans for reading text. Manrope's squared, engineered forms and tabular figures make the specification scan cleanly; DM Sans keeps long technical copy calm at small sizes. Headlines stay modest so the C10 footage leads.

Colour — a graphite-to-silver technical scale with one accent: the blue from The Bionic Eye's own video title card (#008DD2, deepened to #0079B6 for buttons and #006A9E for text). Blue marks interaction and technical highlights only. Dark and light chapters pace the page; all text meets WCAG AA.
