# 08 · Design note (typeface and colour)

<!-- note:start -->
Typeface — Manrope only, in four weights: 600 for headings and figures, 400–500 for reading text, labels and controls. One geometric grotesque keeps the page engineered and calm; the headline stays at 58px so the footage leads, and the five specifications use large lining figures with their qualifiers kept in line.

Colour — graphite surfaces with The Bionic Eye's cyan (#00BFF3) given one broad plane behind the C10's white product stage, plus actions, rules and selected states. Cyan carries dark ink text (7.2:1); links on white use darker cyan (#00758F). All text meets WCAG AA.
