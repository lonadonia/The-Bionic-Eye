# 08 · Design note (typeface and colour)

<!-- note:start -->
Typeface — Archivo, the face The Bionic Eye already uses for its C10 datasheet, so the page and its downloadable specification read as one document. Its variable width axis gives slightly expanded, engineered headlines and condensed, tabular labels for specifications: one family, no decorative pairing.

Colour — the logo's ink (#1D1D1B) and the blue from The Bionic Eye's own video title card (#008DD2, deepened to #006A9E for text), used sparingly as a single signal for indices, focus and active states. Paper and concrete neutrals let authentic site footage carry the colour. All text meets WCAG AA.
