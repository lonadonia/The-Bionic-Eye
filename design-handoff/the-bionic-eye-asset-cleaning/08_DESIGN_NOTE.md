# 08 · Design note (typeface and colour)

<!-- note:start -->
Typeface — Manrope only, in four weights: 600 for headings and figures, 400–500 for reading text, labels and controls. A single geometric grotesque keeps the page engineered and calm; the headline stays at 58px so the footage leads, and the five specifications use large lining figures with their qualifiers kept in line.

Colour — deep graphite surfaces with one cyan accent (#00BFF3) for actions, section rules and selected states. Cyan buttons carry dark ink text (7.2:1); links on white use a darker cyan (#00758F). Light and dark chapters pace the page, and all text meets WCAG AA.
